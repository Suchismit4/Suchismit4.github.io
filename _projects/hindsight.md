Hindsight is research infrastructure for large-scale empirical asset pricing and machine-learning experiments. It brings together JAX/XLA-based pipelines, large financial panels, factor computation, backtesting, and model experimentation.

The focus is the computational infrastructure needed to run and compare research experiments on large datasets.
