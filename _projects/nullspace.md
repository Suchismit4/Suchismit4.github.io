NULLSPACE is an exploratory computational imaging and inverse problems project. It studies measurement operators, reconstruction, null spaces, iterative methods, and learned priors.

The project explores how the measurement operator and the choice of prior affect reconstruction, including the ambiguity introduced by a nontrivial null space.
